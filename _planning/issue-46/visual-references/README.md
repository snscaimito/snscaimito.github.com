## Visual direction

Draw on Venezuelan pageant spectacle and telenovela visual language without copying a real contestant or actor. The mother and daughter should be visibly expressive through their eyes, faces, posture, hands, and interactions. Use saturated color, warm tropical light, purposeful grooming, social proximity, and emotionally charged scenes. Show the mother's middle class origins as cared-for everyday life, her pageant years as a public performance, and her political years as charismatic spectacle.

Avoid muted US corporate portraiture: beige office suits, solitary desk poses, flat expressions, and restrained styling should not define these women. Keep their beauty and family resemblance clear while allowing age, class position, and access to power to change their clothing and presentation.

The mother is confident in her body. At adult ages, give her body-conscious clothing, bare shoulders or a daring neckline when the occasion fits, assured movement, and a gaze that meets other people directly. This should read as mainstream glamour in her world, without explicit nudity, sexual acts, or pornographic camera framing. At fifteen, use age-appropriate clothing and framing; her confidence and beauty need no sexual styling.

Visual research: [Miss Venezuela gala](https://www.missvenezuela.com/noticias/el-brillo-de-la-excelencia-gala-preliminar-miss-universo-venezuela-2025) and [Venevisión telenovela presentation](https://www.venevision.com/nota/dramaticas-se-estrena-el-16-de-octubre-por-venevision).
