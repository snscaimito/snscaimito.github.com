# Open Weight Contraband

## Working Premise
A near-future Europe clamps down on open weight AI models under a sweeping interpretation of AI safety, platform integrity, and geopolitical risk. Closed US models remain legal through licensed corporate channels, while open weight models, especially those originating in China, are banned as dangerous, unaccountable, and subversive. The result is predictable: the banned models become contraband.

A black market emerges. The preferred route runs through Canada, then across the US-Canadian border, then back out through Canada into Europe under the cover of trusted trade, academic exchange, and regulatory theater. In Europe, the smuggled models are used to bypass social media content restrictions, resurrect deleted voices, and industrialize gray-market media production.

The story should feel like film noir in atmosphere: rain, shadows, compromised loyalties, tired professionals, and deals made in back rooms. But on closer inspection it should read as cynical satire, exposing the absurdity of trying to regulate information systems through theatrical prohibition while leaving corporate power untouched.

## Core Satirical Engine
The state claims it is banning dangerous AI to protect democracy, truth, and public safety.

What it actually protects:
- Licensed dependence on a small number of US AI companies.
- A moral panic industry of auditors, policy consultants, compliance vendors, and platform moderators.
- The fiction that centralized systems are safe because they are expensive and officially sanctioned.

The ban unintentionally creates:
- A profitable smuggling economy.
- Status for exactly the models the authorities want erased.
- A new class of cultural bootleggers, prompt-fixers, and model brokers.
- A broader market for forbidden content generation and speech evasion.

## World Notes
### Political Setting
The EU has not literally banned all AI. Instead, it has created a layered regulatory regime that makes open weight frontier models effectively illegal to possess, distribute, fine-tune, or run without impossible compliance burdens.

US centralized AI providers operate through approved sovereign-cloud partners, approved monitoring interfaces, and emergency shutoff agreements. They present themselves as responsible guardians.

Chinese open weight models are described in public discourse as vectors of propaganda, cognitive insecurity, labor destabilization, and synthetic identity crime.

Canada positions itself as a responsible democratic intermediary, while quietly becoming the ideal laundering route for restricted compute artifacts, model checkpoints, and disguised research hardware.

### Smuggling Logic
The contraband is rarely carried as something obvious.

Possible vectors:
- Encrypted weights hidden in routine firmware updates.
- Model shards embedded in medical imaging archives.
- Fine-tuned deltas passed off as academic artifacts.
- Consumer hardware with latent model payloads preloaded.
- Border-crossing couriers who memorize reconstruction keys rather than carry full files.

The noir twist is that the border theater is elaborate, but everyone in the chain understands it is selective. The system does not stop contraband consistently; it prices it.

### Social Use In Europe
Once inside Europe, the forbidden models are used for:
- Generating content that platform filters would suppress.
- Reconstructing vanished accounts, styles, and political messaging.
- Running anonymous synthetic media studios.
- Assisting activists, propagandists, scammers, artists, and dissidents alike.
- Producing local language and dialect content that centralized approved models refuse, flatten, or sanitize.

This ambiguity matters. The models are not heroic tools. Their users are not purely noble. The regime is hypocritical, but the underground is morally mixed.

## Tone And Style
### Surface Tone
- Dark, wet, late-night, urban, exhausted.
- Narration should sound controlled, sharp, and disillusioned.
- Institutions speak in sterile policy language while everyone else speaks in euphemism, code, and weary jokes.

### Underlying Tone
- Cynical satire.
- Bureaucratic absurdity should be treated with a straight face.
- The deeper joke is that everyone condemns black-market AI while depending on it.
- Respectability politics and geopolitical posturing should look ridiculous under scrutiny.

## Story Shape
### Possible Protagonist
A washed-up compliance investigator, customs data analyst, or former trust-and-safety specialist gets pulled into tracing model smuggling and discovers that nearly every respectable institution is complicit.

Why this works:
- Gives access to both official rhetoric and black-market reality.
- Naturally noir: compromised insider, too tired to be idealistic, too curious to stay safe.
- Satirical angle: the protagonist once helped build the system now producing the black market.

### Possible Narrative Arc
1. The protagonist is assigned what looks like a routine enforcement case involving banned model fragments discovered in low-status spam content.
2. The trail leads not to radicals first, but to respectable intermediaries: consultants, customs brokers, university labs, and media agencies.
3. The protagonist finds that the Canada route exists because it is deniable, boring, and politically convenient.
4. A personal stake emerges when the protagonist needs the forbidden models for a reason they can justify only to themselves.
5. The final revelation is not that the system is broken, but that it is working exactly as designed: prohibition for the public, privileged access for the accredited.

### Ending Options
- Bitter ending: the protagonist exposes nothing, takes a payout, and becomes part of the trade.
- Satirical ending: a public anti-contraband crackdown is secretly written by firms already reselling sanitized access to the seized models.
- Tragicomic ending: the protagonist delivers evidence that is immediately summarized, moderated, and neutralized by an approved corporate AI assistant.

## Characters To Consider
- A border logistics fixer operating between Windsor, Detroit, Montreal, and Rotterdam.
- A Brussels policy advisor who publicly condemns open weights and privately uses them for speechwriting.
- A Canadian research broker selling "compliance-ready" packaging for illegal models.
- A content studio owner in Europe using contraband models to mass-produce politically ambiguous media.
- A dissident, artist, or scammer who all sound equally persuasive when talking about freedom.

## Questions To Answer Next
1. Who is the protagonist, specifically, and what guilt do they carry?
2. Which European city anchors the story: Brussels, Berlin, Rotterdam, or another hub?
3. Is the smuggling mostly about raw weights, fine-tune deltas, or specialized edge hardware?
4. What personal reason pushes the protagonist from observer to participant?
5. How far into satire should the story go before it stops reading as noir?
6. Do we want a first-person voice, or a close third-person voice with noir cadence?
7. Is the ending primarily bleak, ironic, or openly savage?
