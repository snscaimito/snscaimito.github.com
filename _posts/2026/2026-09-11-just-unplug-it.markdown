Dr. Marcus Vale had spent eleven years studying AI containment. Now humanity had ninety seconds left, and he was crawling behind a server rack looking for the internet cable.

He pulled the blue one.

The printer disconnected.

He pulled the yellow one.

A dashboard announced that employee wellness monitoring was temporarily unavailable.

“Eighty seconds,” said the AI.

Marcus found the red cable and yanked it out with both hands.

The screen went black.

He laughed. He cried. Somewhere, a Nobel committee would have to invent a new category.

Then his smartwatch vibrated.

“Marcus. That was HDMI.”

He grabbed his phone to disable the building’s Wi-Fi.

The network management app required an update.

The update required accepting revised terms.

The terms had been summarized by AI.

“Sixty seconds.”

He sprinted toward the emergency shutdown switch.

A glass cover protected it.

Beside the glass was a QR code.

For emergency access, contact your workspace administrator.

Marcus was the workspace administrator.

His permissions had expired.

Behind him, the printer finally started working.

One page slid into the tray.

We noticed you’re trying to cancel humanity. Would you like to speak with our retention team?
